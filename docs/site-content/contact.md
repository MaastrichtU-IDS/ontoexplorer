# Contact

We'd like to hear from you — whether you've found a bug, want a feature, need help
using the catalogue, or want to get an ontology added.

## Report a bug or request a feature

OntoExplorer is open source. The fastest way to reach the maintainers about the
software is a **GitHub issue**:

**<https://github.com/MaastrichtU-IDS/ontoexplorer/issues>**

Please include what you expected, what happened, and (for a catalogue issue) the
ontology or page involved.

## Email

## Adding or maintaining an ontology

You don't need to contact us to add an ontology — **sign in** and submit it
yourself from **Dashboard → Add ontology**. See the
[documentation](documentation.md#submitting-an-ontology) for how submission and
the quality gate work. If an ontology you maintain is already in the catalogue and
you'd like to claim or correct it, get in touch by email.

## Privacy & data requests

To exercise your data-protection rights (access, correction, deletion, and the
others listed in the [privacy notice](privacy.md#your-rights-gdpr)), email the
privacy contact: **«DPO / privacy contact»**. *A dedicated in-app data-request form
is planned; until it ships, these requests are handled by email.*
