# Contact

We'd like to hear from you — whether you've found a bug, want a feature, need help
using the catalogue, or want to get an ontology added.

## Report a bug or request a feature

OntoExplorer is open source. The fastest way to reach the maintainers about the
software is a **GitHub issue**:

**<https://github.com/MaastrichtU-IDS/ontoexplorer/issues>**

Please include what you expected, what happened, and (for a catalogue issue) the
ontology or page involved.

## Email

For questions, collaboration, or anything that isn't a public issue, email the
team at **info-ids@maastrichtuniversity.nl**.

Please don't send privacy or data-protection requests here — use the dedicated
channels below.

## The team

OntoExplorer is developed and operated by the **Institute of Data Science (IDS)**
at **Maastricht University** — a research institute working on data science,
knowledge engineering, and FAIR data and services. Learn more about IDS:
<https://www.maastrichtuniversity.nl/research/institute-data-science> *(verify the
institute URL)*.

## Adding or maintaining an ontology

Adding a new ontology, or becoming the maintainer of one already in the catalogue,
is **approved by the OntoExplorer team**. **Sign in**, then request the role from
your dashboard — *uploader* rights to add new ontologies, or *maintainer* rights
over a specific ontology — with a short rationale; an admin approves or declines.
See the [documentation](documentation.md#submitting-an-ontology) for how the
request, submission, and quality gate work. For anything the request flow doesn't
cover, reach us by email.

## Privacy & data-protection requests

OntoExplorer is a service of Maastricht University. To exercise your
data-protection rights (access, correction, deletion, and the others in the
[privacy notice](privacy.md#your-rights-gdpr)):

- Use UM's **GDPR request form**:
  <https://www.maastrichtuniversity.nl/form-gdpr-request> *(verify URL)*, or
- email UM's **GDPR Team** at **privacy@maastrichtuniversity.nl**, or
- email the **Data Protection Officer** at **fg@maastrichtuniversity.nl**.

You may also lodge a complaint with the Dutch supervisory authority, the
**Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens** (<https://www.autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl/en>).
